Six ways to work together

Most portals cover one kind of collaboration. Here, each one starts from the same profile and the same set of templates.

  • Centres of Excellence

    A blueprint covering objectives, equipment, curriculum, staffing and a three-year plan, with partners to build it.

  • Course and lecture delivery

    Self-study, partial-delivery and skill-enhancement courses from startups, mapped to your syllabus.

  • Internships and live projects

    Startups post the skills they need. Departments shortlist students and track credits and feedback.

  • Sponsored projects

    Startups bring a problem, faculty bring the method, and the agreement settles IP and fees up front.

  • Faculty consulting

    Short advisory roles for faculty with clear conflict-of-interest and IP rules.

  • Lab and facility access

    Startups use campus labs, and campuses see who is using what.
